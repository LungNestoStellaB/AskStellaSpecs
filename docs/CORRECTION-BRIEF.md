## ❌ What AskStella IS NOT

**AskStella is NOT:**
- An interactive AI consultation service
- A platform for personalized video responses to individual questions
- A back-and-forth Q&A system where users ask Stella questions and get custom answers
- A HeyGen-powered video chat or consultation service

**Why this model was rejected:**
- The public is ~3 years away from accepting personalized AI consultation
- Too complex, too intimidating for first-time users
- Not scalable for v1 launch

---

## ✅ What AskStella IS

**AskStella is:**
- **"The agent's agent"** - a guide service helping people find THEIR right AI agent
- A recommendation platform for ordinary people asking their first questions about AI agents
- A curated directory (5-8 agents) organized by category
- An educational resource, not a consultation service

**Core promise:** "I'll help you ask the right questions."

**User journey:**
1. User arrives at askstella.online
2. User sees 5 categories (Work, Organization, Creativity, Learning, Everyday Life)
3. User either:
   - Browses categories and reads agent recommendations, OR
   - Submits a question/need via simple form
4. User receives a **guide** (not a personalized video) pointing them to the right agent(s) with clear next steps

**Tone:** Editorial, calm, guide-first. No hype, no jargon. Disarming and professional.

---

## 🎯 What This Means for Your Mission

**For Engineering:**
- Build a **landing page + recommendation interface**, NOT a consultation platform
- Question submission form collects user needs to help route them to the right agent
- No video generation in Phase 1 (HeyGen integration is Phase 2, if needed at all)
- Focus on clean, calm, editorial presentation of curated agents

**For Recon:**
- Research and curate 5-8 agents that serve ordinary people well
- Focus on agents that are accessible, affordable, and proven
- Build routing logic: "If user needs X, recommend Agent Y because..."
